# Review-plan knowledgebase

This directory preserves the research and audit history used to develop the canonical eight-component review plan.

- [`adversarial-review-overview.md`](adversarial-review-overview.md): executive recommendation, evidence labels, and core review terminology.
- [`historical-reviewer-build-recommendation.md`](historical-reviewer-build-recommendation.md): original reviewer consolidation, packaging, and build-order rationale; retained as non-authoritative design history.
- `01-*.md` through `09-*.md`: synthesized guidance and operating recommendations.
- `research/`: source research notes.
- `templates/`: early reviewer templates and reviewer-selection guidance.
- `audit-prompts/`: prompts used to produce or reproduce lifecycle audits.
- `questions/`: design questions developed during the research.
- `audits/`: cold lifecycle audits preserved as historical records.
- `adjudications/`: validation and repair records for the audit findings.

The current normative plan is under [`../canonical/components`](../canonical/components/). The two orchestration designs and the incremental build options are under [`../implementations`](../implementations/).

The audit and adjudication documents are preserved verbatim. Some relative links inside those historical records reflect their original directory layout.
